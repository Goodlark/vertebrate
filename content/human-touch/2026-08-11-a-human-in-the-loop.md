The machines compile the news around here — quickly, tirelessly, and without a
point of view. **This column is where a human puts one back in.**

Every week the feed fills with launches and funding rounds and robots taking their
first steps onto factory floors. What it can't tell you is which of those actually
matters, and why. That's the job here: to argue, to doubt, and occasionally to be wrong
out loud.

Welcome to Human Touch.
