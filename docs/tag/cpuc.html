Waymo's Robotaxi Map Keeps Growing: Denver, Berkeley, Phoenix and California Regulators Sign Off

Waymo continues its city-by-city creep, with rides set to begin soon in Denver and Berkeley and a freshly redesigned sixth-generation vehicle rolling out in Phoenix. California's Public Utilities Commission, meanwhile, cleared that same sixth-gen robotaxi for the Coastside as well as San Jose, San Francisco and Sacramento — a regulatory green light that unlocks yet more turf even as safety questions trail the expansion.
