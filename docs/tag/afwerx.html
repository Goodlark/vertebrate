Joby Aviation's Air Force Autonomy Deal Grows to $37 Million

Joby Aviation expanded its U.S. Air Force autonomous flight contract to nearly $37 million, a boost that followed the company's first autonomous coast-to-coast flight — a Cessna Caravan flown 6,665 miles across the country. The military win coincided with Joby's third-quarter 2026 earnings, in which it raised full-year revenue guidance 15% after completing first test flights in Florida and Texas under a White House-backed air-taxi program.
