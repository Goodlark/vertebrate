Nvidia Quietly Becomes the Chip Inside the Robot Boom

Agility Robotics confirmed its new Digit 5 humanoid will run on Nvidia silicon as the company heads toward a Nasdaq listing, while a separate, unnamed humanoid maker reported an eightfold cut in processing latency using the same Nvidia stack. Meanwhile, AI Magazine traced how Nvidia, test-equipment giant Teradyne, and robotics startup Sereact are stitching together the hardware and software needed to industrialize 'physical AI' at scale. Taken together, the items sketch a company less interested in building robots itself than in making sure every robot that matters needs its chips.
