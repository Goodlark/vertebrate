Bridge Neurotech Raises $13.5M, Partners With Butterfly Network on Non-Invasive BCI

Bridge Neurotech closed a $13.5 million funding round to advance its non-invasive brain-computer interface technology, and simultaneously announced a partnership with ultrasound imaging company Butterfly Network. The collaboration aims to develop a fully non-invasive BCI that could sidestep the surgical implantation requirements that have long limited the field's adoption.
