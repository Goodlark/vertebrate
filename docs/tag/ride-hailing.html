Waymo's Ojai Minivans Go Fully Public, Gain Gemini Brains

Waymo has thrown open its Zeekr-built 'Ojai' robotaxi vans to all riders — no more waitlists — across three cities, and begun accepting fares from paying passengers. The company is also wiring Google's Gemini into the vehicles, letting the AI take on chores beyond mapping, from route chatter to trip planning. It's the clearest sign yet that the vans, once a curiosity, are becoming Waymo's workhorse fleet.

Waymo's Expansion Hits Speed Bumps From Santa Monica to D.C.

As Waymo races to grow its robotaxi fleet — importing thousands of Chinese-made Geely sedans to dodge EV tariffs — it's running into friction on multiple fronts. In Santa Monica, the company is appealing a city ban on overnight charging at two of its stations, while in Washington, D.C., its planned arrival has ride-share drivers bracing for competition. The episodes together sketch a company scaling fast and colliding, city by city, with the people and rules already on the ground.
