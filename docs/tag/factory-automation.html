Boston Dynamics Pushes Deeper Into the Factory Floor

Boston Dynamics is expanding its industrial robotics push on two fronts: opening a training center at Hyundai's Georgia plant to prepare Atlas humanoids for a rollout of 25,000 units across the automaker's factories by decade's end, and releasing Spot 5.2, an upgraded quadruped that ties enterprise software and scattered AI tools into a single stream of site intelligence for autonomous inspections. Together the moves signal the company's ambition to put both legged humanoids and its established Spot line to work on the same shop floors.
