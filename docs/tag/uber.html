Waymo's Fast Growth Runs Into Potholes: A Pedestrian Hit, an Overnight Ban, and Mounting Safety Questions

As Waymo and its robotaxi rivals race to add cities and rides, the messier side of that expansion is surfacing. In Dallas, a pedestrian was struck by two vehicles, one a Waymo car; nationally, reporting shows robotaxi operators are hitting unexpected snags — construction zones, emergency scenes — faster than they can patch them. A comparison of two major U.S. operators found stark safety gaps, with one logging 14 crashes in 800,000 miles despite a human safety driver up front. Meanwhile, a federal judge in San Francisco refused to let Waymo keep running its cars overnight while it appeals a regulatory ban, underscoring how far ahead of oversight the technology is moving.
