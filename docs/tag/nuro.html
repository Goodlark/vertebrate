Uber, Nuro and Lucid Start Driverless Testing of Luxury Robotaxi

Uber, Nuro and Lucid have begun fully driverless testing of their jointly developed luxury robotaxi, dropping safety drivers from the vehicle for the first time. The move positions Lucid's sleek sedan as a genteel challenger to Tesla's robotaxi ambitions, with Nuro supplying the autonomy stack and Uber eyeing eventual fleet deployment.
