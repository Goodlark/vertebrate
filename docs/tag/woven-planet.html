Lyft's Asset-Light Robotaxi Bet Pays Off Across Three Continents

Lyft cashed out of building its own self-driving cars years ago, selling its autonomy unit to Woven Planet rather than burn cash chasing Waymo and Cruise. That patience is now paying dividends: the company is deploying Waymo's driverless vehicles in Nashville while simultaneously rolling out Baidu's robotaxis in London, hedging its bets across rival technology stacks rather than picking a single horse.
