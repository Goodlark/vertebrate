Neuralink Expands Brain-Signal Controls to Calls, Texts and Grok Chatbot

Neuralink has pushed its brain-computer interface beyond cursor control, demonstrating hands-free phone calls, text dictation, and even command of Musk's Grok AI through neural signals alone. In one case, engineers spent 72 straight hours at a study participant's home building automatic voice detection so the patient no longer needs to press a button to start or stop dictating speech with their mind.
