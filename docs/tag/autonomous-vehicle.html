Tesla's Robotaxi Ambitions Meet a Reality Check in Vegas

Tesla is gearing up for an August launch of the Cybercab, a robotaxi built without a steering wheel, even as its existing Robotaxi service hits turbulence on the ground. Nevada's gaming regulator approved the car for Las Vegas streets but slashed the company's requested fleet of 5,000 down to just 10 vehicles, a stark signal of how far Elon Musk's rollout ambitions have outpaced regulators' comfort level.
