Uber's Robotaxi Blitz: 2,000 Pony.ai Cars for Europe, Plus a Tokyo Push

Uber is moving on two fronts at once: a deal with China's Pony.ai to deploy 2,000 robotaxis across Europe, and an expansion into Japan, where a Tokyo pilot is underway. CEO Dara Khosrowshahi called Japan a 'terrific' market for the company, framing the rollout as proof that Uber can scale autonomous rides globally by stacking partnerships rather than building its own self-driving tech. The strategy bets that sheer fleet size and geographic reach can outpace the regulatory and technical snags that have dogged robotaxi rivals.
