Draganfly Pushes Deeper Into Defense Business

Drone maker Draganfly made two moves to bulk up its military credentials: it took a strategic stake in DoD Solution, an Estonian-Ukrainian firm specializing in GPS-denied navigation for unmanned systems, with CEO Cameron Chell joining that company's board, and it spun up a dedicated U.S. defense subsidiary chaired by Thomas Modly, the former acting Secretary of the Navy. Together the steps signal Draganfly's pivot toward selling autonomy tech for contested, jamming-heavy battlefields like Ukraine's.
