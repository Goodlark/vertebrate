Google DeepMind says Gemini Robotics 2 enables full body control

Google DeepMind released Gemini Robotics 2, a model capable of controlling a robot's full body rather than individual limbs.

Why it matters. Most humanoid demos still stitch together scripted single-limb motions; full-body control is the harder, more useful trick, letting a robot coordinate legs, torso and arms to actually do physical work. It puts pressure on Figure, Tesla, and Unitree, who all need this kind of unified control to move past choreographed showcases.

FedEx and Dexterity Expand Physical AI Deployment for Autonomous Trailer Loading at Hagerstown Hub

FedEx and Dexterity are expanding their robotic trailer-loading operation from a pilot site to full production scale at the Hagerstown Hub, deploying Dexterity's Foresight world model to handle a task that has resisted automation.

Why it matters. Trailer loading has resisted automation for years because it demands improvisation with irregular packages in tight, shifting spaces — exactly the kind of task world models like Dexterity's Foresight are built for. Moving from pilot to full production at a real FedEx hub is the proof point warehouse robotics vendors have struggled to produce: physical AI doing dirty, unglamorous logistics work at scale.
