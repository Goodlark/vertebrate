This was the week robotaxis stopped being a Bay Area curiosity and started looking like a national grid, with Waymo and Zoox both expanding into new cities on the same five days. Meanwhile, humanoid robots kept multiplying — as warehouse workers, energy technicians, tour guides, even a college football cheerleader — proof that the hardware is cheap enough now that nobody needs a killer use case to ship one. The quieter, bigger story came from Shanghai, where a brain implant has moved from lab curiosity to insurance line item.
